COMMENTS:
The architectural SDD is generally strong and demonstrates a clear
three-layer architecture with well-defined responsibilities for the web
application, service/data layer, and workstation execution layer.

The architecture is largely consistent with the requirements and provides
good coverage of the major CSCs identified in the SRS.  The strongest
aspects are the clear subsystem decomposition, the architecture diagrams,
the identification of interfaces, and the mapping of requirements to
architectural components.

The design provides a coherent architecture with good subsystem
decomposition, useful diagrams, and strong alignment with the core
functional requirements.  The architecture demonstrates clear thinking
about data ownership, execution separation, security, and layered design.

The strongest sections are the workstation layer, the database interaction
architecture, the simulation lifecycle description, and the architectural
diagrams.  The primary improvement opportunities are expanding support for
protein visualization, operational and performance requirements, multi-GPU
execution architecture, notification architecture, and fault-tolerance
considerations.

Overall Rating: Strong Architectural SDD
